Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know.

Do you provide doorstep repair?

Doorstep service is arranged by appointment. Contact us to confirm your location and the type of repair.

Which phone brands do you support?

We accept enquiries for iPhone and Android devices. Availability depends on the model and fault.

How much does a repair cost?

Price depends on your model, issue and part availability. Send a price enquiry for a quote before proceeding.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your enquiry. Your appointment is confirmed only after you coordinate with us.

Do you provide a repair warranty?

Warranty, if applicable, depends on the repair and parts used. Please confirm the terms before approving the repair.
